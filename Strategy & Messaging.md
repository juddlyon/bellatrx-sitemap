## **Strategy \+ Stakeholders**

*Who we're building for, and the outcomes that matter.*

### **Three visitor paths**

Every visitor lands in one of three buckets. The site's job is to figure out which one and move them forward without friction.

* **Intent:** ready to talk. Book a call, live chat, request a quote.  
* **Interest:** researching. Playbooks, guides, long-form resources, nurture.  
* **Aftermarket:** existing customers. Parts, service, emergency support.

The main conversion everywhere on the site is **book a call**, with a \<15-minute speed-to-lead standard behind it. Secondary conversions: playbook / lead-magnet downloads for Interest visitors, and parts / service requests for Aftermarket.

## **Messaging \+ Market**

*What we say, how we stand apart, who we say it to.*

**Big Idea:** *Your Front of Line Starts Here™*. Category white space; trademark in process. The site is where the campaign lives in permanent form, reinforced on every product page instead of stuck on a single landing page.

### **The sale is the sample**

Blair Enns's idea: how a prospect experiences the website and the sales process is itself a working demo of how BellatRx does business. A frictionless, human, confident, responsive web experience is the first proof of the white glove promise, before anyone's quoted, ordered, or installed a machine. A clunky one undermines every other claim on the page.

### **User as hero**

The visitor's the hero. BellatRx is the guide. We frame copy around the visitor's problem, not BellatRx's offerings. About Us is the only page that's actually about the company; every other page is about what the visitor's trying to solve and what outcome they're after.

### **White glove online**

Elevated, frictionless, consistent. The under-15-minute response sets the standard. The site has to state that expectation up front through CTAs and live up to it invisibly: minimal friction, a clear next step on every page.

### **Visual direction**

* Show *outcomes* (finished packaged products) over machines.  
* Humans and sense of place. B2C-level polish in a category where the bar is steel-on-steel catalogs.  
* Lean into BellatRx's labeled line-integration video style (machine callouts moving through the line); it's both a differentiator and good AEO fuel.  
* Purple as a featured accent for emphasis; it's already a known brand signal at trade shows.

### **Market posture**

* Eight verticals in North America; mid-market (1–4 plants) is the primary sweet spot.  
* The integrated-line story is the wedge; every product page links back to it.  
* Buying teams span 30-year veterans to jargon-light younger buyers; write for both.  
* **Competitive benchmark:** All-Fill is the current bar in packaging machinery. We're here to clear it.
