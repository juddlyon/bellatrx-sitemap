Two things to watch. Purple at its brand value (purple-600, #822FFB) has a 5.6:1 contrast on white — passes AA at every size, so purple links and white-on-purple buttons are fine. It falls just short of AAA (7:1) for small text, so if a client needs AAA on body copy, reach for purple-700. Also, the neutral gray ramp is new (the original set had no working neutral); use it for text, borders and disabled states so we're not inventing grays ad hoc.
